# Decisions

Append only. Product and user-facing decisions: what the app does, shows, or
asks; how a feature behaves; what a skill does for the user. Not code
patterns, libraries, or refactors.

Never edit or delete a line. A decision that changes gets a new line that
says what it replaces (`replaces 2026-09-27 #1`).

Format: `date #n · who · area · decision`. `who` is `user` (said in a
prompt) or `claude` (a tradeoff Claude made; say why). Decisions from the
user are their words turned into a rule, never a guess at what they meant.
